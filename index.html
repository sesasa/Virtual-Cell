    About this model

    Photosynthesis follows the Farquhar–von Caemmerer–Berry model; night-time starch use is paced by the clock; growth follows the Lockhart equation. Pools are in picomoles per cell. Organelle sizes are close to real; molecules are drawn as tokens, each standing for many molecules. Molecules move in real time while the clock runs faster. Growth and the cell cycle run about twice as fast as in a typical leaf so a division fits in the story.
